AesculaMD Institutes

Institutes isn't a separate advisor product built here — it's the free way in to AesculaMD's real, priced advisor platform (roster invites, AI-generated Meeting Briefs before every advising session, cohort-wide analytics, at-risk alerts for students who've gone quiet, action items that sync straight into a student's own tracker, and committee-letter drafting from their actual logged evidence). This bootcamp is the free curriculum layer your students use years before an application cycle; the advisor platform is what your office actually runs on once you're in.

Apply by submitting your student population size, current advising resources, and the specific gaps you want addressed. Approved offices get set up directly in the real product — nothing here is a lightweight stand-in for it.

Community

The honest version: real peer-matching needs real accounts, which this prototype doesn't have yet. Here's what's actually real right now, not a mockup of a cohort that isn't there.

Not built yet — and we'd rather say so than fake it

A real cohort — other students at your exact stage, matched by background and start date, with a real feed and real messages — needs real accounts and a real backend, neither of which this prototype has. Building that honestly, once the rest of this exists, is a real priority. Until then, this page isn't going to show you a "cohort of 24" or a "Thursday Q&A" that don't actually exist.

Real communities, outside this app, you can join today

None of these are part of AesculaMD — they're real, independent, and mostly free:

  • Student National Medical Association (SNMA) — the oldest and largest organization run by and for underrepresented-in-medicine students, with an active pre-med (MAPS) chapter network.
  • Latino Medical Student Association (LMSA) — similar in spirit, with a strong pre-med presence.
  • r/premed — the largest active premed community online. Genuinely useful for real-time questions; treat any single anecdote there with the same healthy skepticism you'd give a stranger's advice anywhere.
  • Student Doctor Network (SDN) forums — one of the longest-running premed and med-student communities, with searchable threads on almost every situation you'll hit.
  • AAMC's Aspiring Docs resources — includes material written specifically for first-generation and low-income applicants, not just generic advice.

What's real, right now, inside this app

Family Conversations in Chat won't replace a real mentor who's known you for years — but it's real, it's always available, and it doesn't require a cohort to exist first.

Privacy & Data

The honest version, not a legal template — what actually happens to what you type here, today, in this prototype.

What actually happens to your reflections right now

This platform has no accounts. Its AI features use a server endpoint. Your real progress — self-assessment ratings, every stage's reflections, your track/testing/ambition choices, and your generated plan — is saved to this browser's local storage, so it survives a refresh and later visits, on this same browser and device. Local storage stays on your device. When you use an AI feature, your question and relevant profile, reflection, or plan context are sent to the server and its AI provider to generate a reply. Do not include identifying patient information. Switching devices, switching browsers, or clearing your browser data all start you over, since there's no account to recover from. Chat messages and the Institutes application still only live in this tab's memory and are lost on refresh. You can clear everything saved here yourself, anytime, with the button below.

Why this page exists before that changes

Several stages ask for genuinely personal material — family financial or immigration circumstances, health history, caregiving responsibilities, moments of real doubt. AI requests already transmit the relevant context described above. Before adding cloud accounts or institutional sharing, the platform needs a published policy — covering who can see it, how long it's kept, and whether an advising office that licenses Institutes content can see individual students' reflections or only aggregate, anonymized patterns. None of those decisions have been made yet, which is exactly why this page doesn't claim to have already made them.

What this means if you're evaluating this for a school or program

If you're a pre-health advising office considering an Institutes pilot: treat the absence of a real privacy policy as a genuine open item to resolve before any real student data is involved, not as a settled "no problem here." We'd rather say that plainly than have it surface later as a surprise. Worth knowing too: if your office is approved, your actual onboarding and student data happen inside AesculaMD's own advisor platform at aesculamd.com, which has its own separate, real privacy practices — not inside this prototype. This page is only ever describing what's true here.
