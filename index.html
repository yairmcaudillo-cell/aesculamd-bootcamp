Community

The honest version: real peer-matching needs real accounts, which this prototype doesn't have yet. Here's what's actually real right now, not a mockup of a cohort that isn't there.

Not built yet — and we'd rather say so than fake it

A real cohort — other students at your exact stage, matched by background and start date, with a real feed and real messages — needs real accounts and a real backend, neither of which this prototype has. Building that honestly, once the rest of this exists, is a real priority. Until then, this page isn't going to show you a "cohort of 24" or a "Thursday Q&A" that don't actually exist.

Real communities, outside this app, you can join today

None of these are part of AesculaMD — they're real, independent, and mostly free:

  • Student National Medical Association (SNMA) — the oldest and largest organization run by and for underrepresented-in-medicine students, with an active pre-med (MAPS) chapter network.
  • Latino Medical Student Association (LMSA) — similar in spirit, with a strong pre-med presence.
  • r/premed — the largest active premed community online. Genuinely useful for real-time questions; treat any single anecdote there with the same healthy skepticism you'd give a stranger's advice anywhere.
  • Student Doctor Network (SDN) forums — one of the longest-running premed and med-student communities, with searchable threads on almost every situation you'll hit.
  • AAMC's Aspiring Docs resources — includes material written specifically for first-generation and low-income applicants, not just generic advice.
