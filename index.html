AesculaMD Institutes

Institutes is a custom-content licensing program for pre-health advising offices, HBCU pipeline programs, and health equity nonprofits. Your students get the same stage-gated bootcamp and agent structure — with your curriculum, your voice, and your local partnerships built in.

Apply by submitting your student population size, current advising resources, and the specific gaps you want Institutes to fill. All applicants receive a fit assessment once review is complete.

School List

Detailed, school-by-school comparisons live in the full AesculaMD platform, not the bootcamp — here's why, and what to focus on instead.

This lives in the full AesculaMD platform

Real Reach/Target/Safety comparisons against the full, current school database — not an illustrative sample — are part of AesculaMD's full application-cycle platform. Once you're deep enough into your cycle to need that level of detail, that's where it lives.

Bootcamp stays focused on the earlier part of the journey: building your case, not managing your application list. Ask the School Fit agent in Chat if you want to talk through list-building strategy in the meantime.
