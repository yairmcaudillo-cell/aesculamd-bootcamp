# Learning Studio
The Studio complements the ten deep-dive stages: principle → fictional decision case → explanatory feedback → personal reflection → observable next action. Practice completion is never an admissions readiness score.

Canonical teaching intentions: evidence over titles; investigate motivation through encounters; plan around access constraints; verify program-specific requirements; diagnose academic difficulties; examine clinical duties and supervision; listen to community needs; value scientific inquiry over publication promises; lead through useful improvements; revise plans around capacity.

Source framework: https://students-residents.aamc.org/real-stories-demonstrating-premed-competencies/premed-competencies-entering-medical-students (read September 19, 2026). Cases are original examples, not AAMC questions or endorsed material. Program requirements must be checked directly. Existing deep lessons retain their source panels.

Learning methods: case decisions, explanatory feedback, self-explanation, reflection-to-action transfer, and spaced revisits. Successful retrieval is revisited after 1, 3, 7, then 14 days. Incorrect answers return after one day. These intervals are product choices, not a validated algorithm. Case completion never silently completes a deep-dive stage.

Personalization: current priority, weekly capacity, year, application year, and access constraints drive three small actions with transparent reasons and estimates. Completion and rescheduling persist. Nothing contacts an organization for the student. AI is separately labeled and invoked on request with a disclosure of the data sent.
